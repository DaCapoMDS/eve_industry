What do the columns mean?
Profit/batch: T2 items = one successful invention → full BPC manufactured and sold (after invention failures); BPO items = one job of . After materials, job costs, tax and broker fee.
ROI @avg: ROI if you only get the region's 30-day average price — the conservative number.
Vol/day: average over the last 30 days without the single biggest day, so one dump doesn't inflate it.
Time: ⚒ manufacturing hours per batch; 🔬 lab hours per successful BPC (copy + all invention attempts incl. failures) — with your Industry, Advanced Industry and Science skills and the blueprint's TE.
ISK/hour: profit per hour of manufacturing slot, and below per hour of lab slot — your slots are the limit, so this shows which items use them best.
Sell-thru: days of that volume needed to sell one batch (if you were the only seller). Illiquid = more than days.
Ships / Mineral Builds tabs: ISK per Tritanium = what each Tritanium earns when built into the item (sale after fees − other minerals − job cost, ÷ Tritanium used). Green = better than selling the Tritanium raw at Amarr; ×2.0 = twice the Tritanium price.
Market supply: everything all sellers have listed at Amarr ÷ a typical day's sales = days until sold out. Low = little competition, high = flooded market.
10% share/day: profit per day if you sell 10% of a typical day's volume, at the lower of today's price and the 30-day average.
I have: your units in hangar + on your sell orders + in running jobs (hover for the split) and how many days of market volume that is; green = on your sell orders.
Hide: BPO not for sale = T1 items whose blueprint nobody sells in the region and you don't own (the game data lists them, but you can't easily get the BPO).
Hide: missing skills = T2 items where an invention skill isn't trained (red "needs …" tag) — you can't invent those yet.
Show only: can build now = items you own the BPO for or hold an invented T2 blueprint copy for (your current build list).
Source: where the item comes from; ✓ 2 BPC · 20 runs = invented blueprints you already hold, inventing = invention running.
